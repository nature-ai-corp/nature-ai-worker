# NATURE AI CORP

**Build, Govern, Deploy and Operate Enterprise AI at Scale.**

NATURE AI CORP develops practical AI products for business operations. Our open-source work makes general-purpose AI tools easier to deploy and use, while enterprise Platform implementation and business data stay private.

### Open-source projects

**[Nature AI Worker](https://github.com/nature-ai-corp/nature-ai-worker)** — a lightweight, self-hosted AI worker that uses local models or cloud AI providers, works on files in a local workspace, and requires approval for sensitive actions.

- [Windows v0.1.1 download](https://github.com/nature-ai-corp/nature-ai-worker/releases/tag/v0.1.1)
- [Source code and setup instructions](https://github.com/nature-ai-corp/nature-ai-worker)
- [Report an issue](https://github.com/nature-ai-corp/nature-ai-worker/issues)
- [Contribute](https://github.com/nature-ai-corp/nature-ai-worker/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)

### Open-source and enterprise boundaries

Nature AI Worker is distributed under Apache-2.0. NATURE AI Platform Server, enterprise data, proprietary business implementation and private infrastructure are **not** included in this open-source repository. Published client integration contracts do not disclose enterprise server code.

### Community

Contributions, bug reports and documentation improvements are welcome through the individual project's GitHub repository.
